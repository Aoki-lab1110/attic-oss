Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

ATTIC ("the App") is an iOS media player provided by an individual developer ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Our approach

The App does not send your videos, viewing history, settings or any other information to the Developer's servers. The Developer does not collect or keep this information. The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools or tracking.

2. Information stored on your device

  • References to the videos and folders you add (file references, NAS and server URLs)
  • Folders you organize, playback positions, playback history and thumbnails
  • Copies of videos you choose to save
  • Protected-folder settings and the data used to check your PIN
  • Connection settings and credentials for program-guide servers
  • App settings

This information is kept in the App's storage or in the iOS Keychain. The App never modifies the original files you pick in the Files app or elsewhere.

3. Network connections

The App makes the following connections in response to what you do. None of them go through the Developer's servers.

  • Finding NAS and media servers: the App looks for devices on your local network.
  • Servers you specify: the App fetches lists, videos and program information from the NAS, HTTP sources and program-guide servers you set up, including through VPNs such as Tailscale.
  • Wi-Fi transfer: only while the transfer screen is open, the App runs a temporary server on your device so a computer on the same network can send videos to it. Connecting requires a one-time code shown on the screen, and the server stops when you close the screen or lock the device. Deleting copies from the computer works only if you allow it on that screen.

4. Device features the App uses

FeaturePurpose
Local networkFinding and connecting to NAS and media servers; Wi-Fi transfer
Face ID / Touch IDUnlocking protected folders. iOS performs the authentication; the App never receives biometric data.
File and photo pickersReading the files, images and videos you choose. The App can access only what you pick.
SharingReceiving videos shared from other apps

You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app. Some features stop working if you turn a permission off.

5. Protected folders

Protected folders restrict viewing and access within the App with a PIN or biometric authentication. Saved files are covered by iOS Data Protection; the App does not add its own encryption.

6. Backups

Copies of videos saved from a NAS or other server can be downloaded again, so they are excluded from device backups such as iCloud Backup. Other App data may be included in Apple backups, depending on your device settings.

7. Apple services

The App may use the App Store (purchases and restores), iCloud Backup and standard iOS features. Apple's privacy policy governs how information is handled by these services.

8. Sharing with third parties

The Developer does not hold your information, and therefore does not share, sell or rent it to third parties.

9. Deleting data

You can delete copies and registrations within the App. Deleting the App removes the information stored in it from your device; however, due to how iOS works, information stored in the Keychain may remain on the device. Original files and videos on your NAS are never deleted.

10. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when features are added or laws change. Revisions take effect when they are posted on this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's update notes.

12. Contact

Please use the contact form (GitHub). Submissions are public, so do not include personal information such as your email address.

If the Japanese and English versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.
